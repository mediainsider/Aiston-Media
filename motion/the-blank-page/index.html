Where most of us start
Don’t start by writing down everything you want to tell them.
HOW DO I WANT
THEM TO FEEL?
Step 1
Take a blank piece of paper.
Step 2
At the top, write:
How do I want them to feel?
Step 2
Then write your answers underneath.
Whatever feels right for your situation.
Step 3
Before you say anything, look at those words.
Then ask yourself
“
Are the words I’m about to use likely to make them feel this way?
That’s a very different way to prepare
for a difficult conversation.
Because it moves the focus…
Away from
“How do I make them change?”
Towards
“How do I create a conversation where change might become possible?”
There is a big difference.
